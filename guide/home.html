Will it play my character?

Bring a D&D Beyond fifth-edition sheet that opens by link while signed out. The dial uses the 2014 or 2024 version of each supported class feature and spell, including on mixed sheets.

Which dial do I need?

This firmware is built and tested for the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Knob-Touch-LCD-1.8 with its 1.8-inch round touchscreen and rotary ring. Other boards, including other ESP32-S3 models, need a hardware port before they can run it.
