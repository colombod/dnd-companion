Check your dial

Connect your Waveshare ESP32-S3-Knob-Touch-LCD-1.8 with a USB data cable. The plug at the dial selects which of its two processors the computer sees.

Find the main USB port

Choose Check my dial. In the browser list, select USB JTAG/serial debug unit (also called Espressif USB Serial/JTAG), then Connect.

One-time USB update

The checker selects the update for your installed build. Your characters, artwork and Wi-Fi settings stay on the dial.

Empty port list?

Close the list. Unplug at the dial, turn the plug over 180°, and plug it back in. Leave the computer end connected. Choose Check my dial again.

Already tried both ways?

Use a cable that can transfer files and connect directly to the computer. Close Arduino Serial Monitor or other apps using the port, then try again in desktop Chrome or Edge.

Checking the cable is read-only. Installation requires a separate confirmation.
